Privacy Notice

CONVAD (Academic Prototype) — Last updated: 24 January 2026

This notice explains how CONVAD processes personal data. If you do not agree, do not use the Service.

1. Data Controller

The Service is operated as an academic project. The Data Controller is the project owner (student developer), for academic research and evaluation purposes.

2. Categories of Personal Data

  • Conversation content you submit
  • Message metadata (timestamps, message role labels)
  • Pseudonymous identifiers (e.g., user ID or session ID)
  • Inferred conversational topics/intent signals used for contextual advertisement matching (profiling)

The Service does not intentionally collect special category data. Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal information.

3. Purposes of Processing

  • Provide conversational functionality and continuity
  • Infer real-time conversational context for contextual advertisement selection
  • Maintain system reliability (e.g., debugging, monitoring)
  • Evaluate system performance in an academic context

4. Legal Basis

Processing is conducted under Legitimate Interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) for operating and evaluating the prototype, and/or Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) where applicable depending on the deployment design.

5. Profiling and Automated Processing

The Service performs automated analysis of conversational text to derive high-level contextual signals for advertisement matching. This is intended to be session-limited, not intended to produce legal or similarly significant effects, and not intended to build persistent behavioural profiles.

6. Data Sharing

Personal data is not sold. Data is not shared with third-party advertising networks by default. Limited access may occur only where required for hosting or database operation, subject to appropriate safeguards.

7. International Transfers

If hosting infrastructure is located outside the UK/EEA, international transfers may occur. Appropriate safeguards would be required depending on the deployment environment.

8. Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary to operate the Service and/or support academic evaluation. Data may be deleted or anonymised after the project evaluation period.

9. Security Measures

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are implemented to protect data (e.g., access controls, least-privilege, secure configuration). As an academic prototype, enterprise-grade guarantees cannot be assured.

10. Your Rights

  • Access, correction, deletion
  • Restriction or objection to processing
  • Withdrawal of consent where consent is used
  • Data portability where applicable

11. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact the project owner through the supervising academic institution.
